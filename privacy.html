Privacy Policy — Ujiue Clips Uploader

Effective date: November 1, 2026

1. About the app

Ujiue Clips Uploader is a personal, single-user tool operated by the TikTok creator @ujiue_tanpen for their own TikTok account only. It is not offered to other users.

2. What the app does

The app uses TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API (video.upload) only to upload the operator's own edited videos to the operator's own TikTok inbox as drafts. The operator then reviews each draft in the TikTok app and publishes it manually.

3. Data handled

The app handles only the following:

  • TikTok OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens for the operator's own account. These are stored securely and used only to call the TikTok API.
  • The video files the operator chooses to upload. These are sent to TikTok.

4. No sale or sharing of data

No personal data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties. The app uses no advertising and no analytics trackers.

5. Revoking access

The operator can revoke the app's access at any time from the TikTok account settings, which stops all app access immediately.
