Ujiue Clips Uploader

Ujiue Clips Uploader is a personal, single-user tool operated by the TikTok creator @ujiue_tanpen. It uploads the operator's own edited videos to the operator's own TikTok account inbox as drafts via the official TikTok API. The operator then reviews each draft in the TikTok app and publishes it manually.

The app is not a public service and is not distributed to other users.
